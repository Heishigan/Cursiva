ABOUT THE ROLE 📌

As an AI Engineer, you play a key role in strengthening and scaling Stratsys’ AI capabilities. The role combines software engineering with practical AI development, focusing on building systems and services around modern AI technologies rather than developing models from scratch.

You will be part of a small AI-focused team working closely together across several ongoing initiatives. Collaboration, experimentation, and shared ownership are central parts of how the team works, with a strong focus on continuously evolving AI capabilities across the organization.

The role includes both hands-on development and a more exploratory way of working where you test and evaluate new AI-related approaches, explore what works in practice and collaborate closely with stakeholders to identify relevant use cases and solutions.

You will also support other development teams within the organization as a technical sounding board around AI features and AI-related initiatives. This can include discussions around implementation approaches, technical feasibility, and how AI capabilities can be integrated in a sustainable and scalable way.

Key parts of the role include:

Developing and maintaining AI-driven services and solutions, primarily in Python

Building systems and functionality around AI models

Collaborating closely with other development teams around AI-related initiatives and features

Acting as a technical advisor and sounding board around AI solutions and implementations

Exploring and evaluating new technologies, methods, use cases and contributing to scaling Stratsys AI initiatives

Collaborating with stakeholders across the business to identify opportunities where AI can create value
